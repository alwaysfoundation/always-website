# Trademark policy

"Always", "Always Foundation", "Always Network" and the Always mark (two paths joining into one road) are trademarks held for the members of the Always Foundation. Open source does not include the brand: this keeps people safe from look-alike services that might mishandle someone's faith story.

## You may, without asking
- Say your app or service is "built on Always", "connects to the Always Network" or "uses the Always Handoff standard", in plain text.
- Use the name to refer to the project accurately in articles, talks and research.
- Fork the code. A fork must use a different name and logo.

## Please ask first (hello@alwaysfoundation.org)
- Using the Always mark or logo.
- Any use that could suggest the foundation endorses, verifies or runs your product.
- Using "Always" in a product, domain or organisation name.

## Never
- Present an unverified church or service as "Always verified".
- Use the name or mark on anything that collects people's details outside the Always consent rules.

Until incorporation, Rejoice holds any registrations on trust for the foundation and will assign them to it.
