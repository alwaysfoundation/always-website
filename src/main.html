The Global Harvest

From yes to baptism, together.

Who they are

The Global Harvest is an Australian network helping everyday believers make disciples who make disciples, right where God has placed them. Each Good Friday, hundreds are baptised at their gatherings in several cities.

Training, one tap away

Always Sent now has a home for their tools, from the 4 Fields to the 3 Circles and the 15 Second Testimony. Each opens on their own site, credited to them, so the training stays theirs and Christians find it when they need it.

A shared way to ask about baptism

Anyone can say they’re ready to be baptised. They choose who helps: a local partner church, The Global Harvest, or both, each by its own tick. Young people go only to a safe local church with a parent’s okay, and everyone’s details stay in Australia.

We’re early in walking together, and grateful to be.

Gospel conversations that lead somewhere.

Always Reports

No one lost between ministries.

The problem

Someone says yes to Jesus at an event, does a course with another ministry and joins a local church. Each one helps, but none of them knows it’s the same person, so it’s easy for someone to slip away.

How journeys work

With the person’s yes, Always links their steps. Each ministry gets a different code, so they can’t use those codes to connect records. The church that welcomes them sees earlier steps by month, never notes. Everyone else sees totals.

Built on an established approach

Journeys use privacy-preserving record linkage, the same idea health services and researchers rely on to recognise one patient across hospitals without merging their records. Each ministry keeps its own records. Always turns a mobile number into a protected fingerprint only Always can match, and gives each ministry a different code for the same person, so no one can join lists behind their back. Only steps and months are shared, never notes.

How we’re trialling it

Carefully, and small first:

  • Adults only, and only people who tick a separate box to link their journey.
  • A small group of ministries first, at events where people already respond through Always.
  • Totals first: we check counts are right and small groups stay hidden before any church sees earlier steps.
  • An independent privacy and security review before it opens more widely.
  • Anyone can withdraw at any time, and their journey is unlinked straight away.

UEN

UEN meets young people where they play.

Who UEN is

UEN, the Universal eSports Network, is a Christian gaming ministry founded by Jake Wilson. Its events are safe and welcoming, online and in person, and every one shares the faith. UEN goes where young Australians already are.

Helping build Always Platform

UEN helped found Always Platform and shape its design. We tried Always Platform at UEN's youth events: young people who said yes were offered a next step near home, with their permission and a leader checking every introduction.

Jake Wilson, UEN's founder, calls Always Platform the air between ministries: it carries a young person from the event where they met Jesus to their next step.

Rejoice

Safety and spiritual impact, across every app.

Who Rejoice is

Rejoice is an Australian Christian technology company building apps for churches, creators and families.

Across its consumer apps

Rejoice uses Always Safety and Always Reports for spiritual impact across its consumer app portfolio. Each app draws on the same shared foundation instead of carrying that work alone, so the Rejoice team can give more of its energy to serving people well.

Contributing to the platform

Rejoice is also a contributor to Always Platform. What it builds and learns flows back into shared tools that every church and ministry can use freely.

CXMMXNS · Coming in 2027

CXMMXNS takes worship across Australia.

The tour

CXMMXNS is planning a worship tour of cities across Australia in 2027, with nights of worship and the Gospel.

How Always Platform will serve it

Every night, people who say yes to Jesus can respond on their phones from a QR code on the screens. They choose a next step and, with their permission, are connected with a local church in their own city. The tour team gets a simple report for each city, with totals only.

Image: CXMMXNS.

Why we exist

No one who says yes to Jesus should walk alone.

Every week across Australia, people respond to Jesus at concerts, camps, services, on the street and online. Too many never find their way into a church family.

Churches and ministries are already doing the work. We serve them quietly, behind the scenes, with shared tools, so every yes has a next step and every team has more time for ministry.

We're new, and grateful to be serving alongside churches and ministries already using these tools.
