No one who responds to Jesus should walk alone.

Jesus sent His Church to make disciples. When someone responds to Him, we make sure they're offered their next best faith step, whether that's a local church, a course to explore faith, discipleship or care, and a real person walks with them.

Every response deserves a next step.

The moment someone says yes to exploring or following Jesus shouldn't be the moment they become disconnected. Here's how we help.

01 / Respond

Someone responds to Jesus.

At a service, camp, event or online, they choose what would help them take their next step.

02 / Connect

They're connected to someone local.

They see who will reach out before sharing anything. Often a church, sometimes a ministry.

03 / Walk

A real person walks with them.

A human welcome, and a clear path towards discipleship, community and care.

Go ye therefore, and teach all nations… and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.

Our name comes from that promise. Jesus told us to make disciples, not just gather decisions.

Every week people respond to Jesus at concerts, camps, services and online. Too often, no one follows up. We want every one of them welcomed, prayed for and walked with.

1606

Austrialia del Espíritu Santo

The Great South Land of the Holy Spirit.

In 1606 the explorer Pedro Fernández de Quirós named the land he found the southern land of the Holy Spirit. He had reached what is now Vanuatu, but the name and the prayer behind it have stirred Australian Christians ever since.

We share that prayer: that by the Spirit, this land would live up to its name, and every Australian would hear the Gospel and be walked with.

That's why Always is Australia first: built here, for the Church here, with people's information kept in Australia under Australian privacy law. The technology is open source, so the Church in other nations is free to use it too.

Case study

UEN meets young people where they play.

Who UEN is

UEN, the Universal eSports Network, is a Christian ministry in the esports and gaming world. It runs in-person and online gaming events that are safe, welcoming and free of toxicity, and every one includes a faith element. Founded by Jake Wilson, UEN goes where young Australians already spend their time.

Founding member and design partner

UEN is a founding member of the Always Foundation and our design partner. Always was trialled at UEN's youth events: young people who responded were offered a next best faith step near where they live, with their agreement and a leader checking every introduction. What UEN learned in those rooms shaped how Always works.

Jake Wilson, Founder and CEO of UEN, describes Always as the air between ministries: what lets a young person move from the event where they met Jesus to the next best faith step on their journey.
