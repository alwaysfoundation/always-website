Case study

UEN meets young people where they play.

Who UEN is

UEN, the Universal eSports Network, is a Christian gaming ministry founded by Jake Wilson. Its events are safe and welcoming, online and in person, and every one shares the faith. UEN goes where young Australians already are.

Helping build Always

UEN helped found Always and helped design it. We tried Always at UEN's youth events: young people who said yes were offered a next step near home, with their permission and a leader checking every introduction. What UEN learned shaped how Always works.

Jake Wilson, UEN's founder, calls Always the air between ministries: it carries a young person from the event where they met Jesus to their next step.
