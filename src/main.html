Every week Australians hear the Gospel and say yes to Jesus. Too often, no one follows up, and they never find a church family.

Our name comes from Jesus' promise in the Great Commission: “lo, I am with you alway.” He sent us to make disciples, not just count decisions. We want every person who says yes to be welcomed, walked with and worshipping.

UEN

UEN meets young people where they play.

Who UEN is

UEN, the Universal eSports Network, is a Christian gaming ministry founded by Jake Wilson. Its events are safe and welcoming, online and in person, and every one shares the faith. UEN goes where young Australians already are.

Helping build Always Platform

UEN helped found Always Platform and shape its design. We tried Always Platform at UEN's youth events: young people who said yes were offered a next step near home, with their permission and a leader checking every introduction.

Jake Wilson, UEN's founder, calls Always Platform the air between ministries: it carries a young person from the event where they met Jesus to their next step.

Photos: UEN, used with permission.

Rejoice

Rejoice builds its apps on Always Platform.

Who Rejoice is

Rejoice is an Australian Christian technology company. It makes apps for churches, creators and families, and its apps use Always Platform to offer people a next step.

Contributing to Always Platform

Rejoice is a contributor to Always Platform. It also uses the platform's reporting and compliance tools to meet Australian privacy and child safety standards for the churches it serves.

“The Always Foundation is fully open source because the Church should own its tools, and every church should stay free to lead its own people.”

Daniel Dizon, CEO and Co-founder, Rejoice

Photo: Rejoice, used with permission.

Sydney Fire Evangelism

Sydney Fire takes the Gospel to the streets.

Who Sydney Fire is

Sydney Fire Evangelism is a team of street evangelists. They share Jesus in public places across Sydney, with worship, preaching and prayer.

Live with Always Platform

We're running Always Platform live in a ministry setting with Sydney Fire, to connect churches. Their team has its own app, with their logo, at sydneyfire.alwaysfoundation.org. People who say yes scan a QR code, choose a next step and, with their permission, their details are kept securely in Australia so the team can follow up and help them find a church.

Photo: Sydney Fire Evangelism.

Alpha

Routing people to Alpha to explore faith.

Who Alpha is

Alpha is a series of sessions, in person or online, that gives people a safe, honest space to explore life, faith and meaning. Churches across Australia run it, and everyone is welcome.

How Always Platform routes to Alpha

Always Platform includes an Alpha integration. When someone who has just heard the Gospel chooses "Explore faith" in an outreach form, like Sydney Fire Evangelism's, they're routed to Alpha and can find a course near them straight away. If they agree, their first name, mobile and suburb are passed to Alpha, so the Alpha team can help them join a course.

Photo and logo: Alpha Australia, used with permission.

CXMMXNS · Coming in 2027

CXMMXNS takes worship across Australia.

The tour

CXMMXNS is planning a worship tour of cities across Australia in 2027, with nights of worship and the Gospel.

How Always Platform will serve it

Every night, people who say yes to Jesus can respond on their phones from a QR code on the screens. They choose a next step and, with their permission, are connected with a local church in their own city. The tour team gets a simple report for each city, with totals only.

Image: CXMMXNS.
