For the Sydney Fire team

When someone says yes, let them scan this, or hand them your phone.

  • 1/ Pray with them first. Celebrate their yes. The form can wait a minute.
  • 2/ Let them choose. They pick their next step and say yes to being connected. Never fill it in for them.
  • 3/ Under 15? A parent or guardian says yes too. Under 13, the parent fills it in.
  • 4/ Stay with them. If it doesn't send, take their name the way you usually do, and let us know.
  • 5/ Follow up. Every contact is saved securely in Australia. The Always Foundation shares new contacts with your team leader for follow-up.
